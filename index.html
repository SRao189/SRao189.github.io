Fig. 1 Intel's climate goals, checked

  1. Baseline. A generic timeline of "focus areas" with no quantified performance data.
  2. Intervention. Rebuilt as a data story: Intel's five public deadlines set against Intel's own 2021–2025 figures, with one plain status per goal.
  3. Result. Every yearly performance figure comes from page 9 of Intel's 2025–26 report, and the one computed number is shown with its formula. Charts are drawn from tables that stay readable without JavaScript. Yearly figures are sourced to Intel's published performance table; commit 5a03892 records checks of all 20 values and the timeline scale.

Fig. 3 Fran's Flowers

  1. Baseline. A centered template for a fictional Mission District florist: an oval photo and soft default colors.
  2. Intervention. An identity taken from the shop's own photo: the pink stucco wall, the teal door, the marigold pots. Specials hang as price tags in a shop window.

Fig. 4 A check-in desk for a summit

  1. Baseline. A glowing orb and identical cards. On a phone the form sat below the fold, and the scores were hard to read from across a table.
  2. Intervention. A registration-desk scoreboard: 50 seats fill in team colors in arrival order. One-tap team tiles, plus staff tools for undoing a mistake.
  3. Result. The original logic and its self-test were kept, and a scripted browser run of 23 check-ins, an undo and a reload passed at phone and desktop widths. An independent review is still pending.

Method

Every revision follows this protocol, in this order. Where a step is still open, its figure says so.

  1. Freeze the baseline. The graded version is tagged in git before anything changes, so panel a is always recoverable.
  2. Write the brief. Who uses the page, what they need from it, and which facts must stay true.
  3. Replace filler with sources. Numbers come from the primary document, cited down to the page.
  4. Measure. Rendered and checked in a real browser at 320, 390 and 1440 px, with reduced motion on and off, and no console errors.
  5. Check against the brief. Review sources, wording, accessibility and behavior; record corrections and unresolved limitations.

The original submissions are preserved. These later revisions document the design decisions, sources, corrections and remaining limitations. Sahar Rao is responsible for what ships.
