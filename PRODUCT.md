# Sahar Rao: web work

register: brand
mode: experience

## What it is
A one-page portfolio that shows four rebuilt class websites as before/after evidence. Each panel **a** is the graded original, preserved at a `submitted-original` git tag; each panel **b** is the revision.

## Who it is for
Lab PIs, research staff and program coordinators who got a link from Sahar and have about a minute. They are deciding whether Sahar can build or fix a lab or project website carefully.

## What they should believe
Sahar works like an experimentalist: freeze a baseline, change one thing deliberately, measure, and say what is still unverified.

## Product truth
- Sahar Rao is a neuroscience student at UC Riverside.
- The four projects, their live URLs, their repos and their tags are real.
- The revisions were made with AI assistance (Claude Code), and the page says so.
- Open items: the Arabic edition has not been reviewed by a native speaker, and the summit check-in has not yet had an independent review.

## Visual world
A journal figure plate: panel letters, figure numbers, scale bars, captions that read like figure legends. White ground (chroma 0), near-black ink, and one violet accent (DAPI stain) for the divider and live state. Schibsted Grotesk.

## Anti-references
Dark neon portfolio with glowing cards; cream-and-serif editorial; a skills grid with progress bars.
