## What it is
An experimental creative studio hub (`UNFINISHED FUTURES`) that presents four rebuilt class websites as verifiable before/after evidence. Each panel **a** is the graded original preserved at a `submitted-original` git tag; each panel **b** is the rebuilt system.

## Who it is for
Lab PIs, research staff, creative technologists, and program coordinators who have about a minute to judge whether Sahar can design and engineer an original, rigorous web system without sacrificing factual or clinical precision.

## What they should believe
Sahar works like an experimentalist and creative director: freeze a baseline (`panel a`), break the frame deliberately, rebuild the system (`panel b`), measure at `320 / 390 / 1440 px`, and state openly what is still unverified.

## Product truth
- Sahar Rao is a neuroscience student at UC Riverside (University Honors, clinical EMT).
- The four projects, their live URLs, their repos, and their `submitted-original` git tags are real.
- The opening **Digital Sculpture** (*Fracture to Framework*) is a procedural Three.js + SVG thesis object whose **12 tectonic facets** act as a design metaphor for the **3 stages (Baseline, Intervention, Result) across the 4 projects**, fracturing along outward face normals and recomposing into 4 portal columns above the figure plates (`Fig. 1`–`Fig. 4`).
- The original submissions are preserved. These later revisions document the design decisions, sources, corrections and remaining limitations. Sahar Rao is responsible for what ships.
- Open items: the Arabic edition has not yet been reviewed by a native speaker, and the summit check-in awaits an independent review.

## Visual world
- **80% Neutral / 20% Accent**:
  - **Opening Specimen Chamber (`Fracture to Framework`)**: Obsidian studio ink (`--studio-ink`, `#0b0c10`), warm chalk typography (`--chalk`, `#f7f6f2`), smoked-glass and matte-gunmetal 3D facets (`roughness >= 0.42`), DAPI violet (`--violet`, `#5b3df5`) internal resin core, and sparse acid (`--acid`, `#d4ff00`) hairline cleavage seams and interactive telemetry ticks.
  - **Disciplined Figure Plates (`Fig. 1`–`Fig. 4`)**: Disruptive opening resolves into a high-contrast white journal figure ground (`--ground`), near-black ink (`--ink`), scale bars, and DAPI violet (`--stain`) comparison dividers.
- **Three-Tier Typography**: Self-hosted `Schibsted Grotesk` (`900` uppercase display + `400–700` editorial prose) paired with monospace technical telemetry (`tabular-nums` restricted to monospace/numerals).

## Anti-references
- Generic floating blob or rainbow-gloss 3D spheres
- Glowing neon rave-brochure cards
- Unpinned or heavy 3D downloads forced onto mobile or reduced-motion visitors (the 690 KB `three.module.min.js` bundle is strictly gated to `min-width: 900px` with `pointer: fine` and `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; mobile and reduced-motion visitors get the zero-weight interactive SVG architectural diagram).
