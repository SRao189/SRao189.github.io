# srao189.github.io

Sahar Rao's web portfolio (`UNFINISHED FUTURES · Make. Break. Remake.`): four rebuilt class websites, shown as journal-style before/after figures. Panel **a** is the graded original, preserved at each repo's `submitted-original` tag. Panel **b** is the revision.

Live: https://srao189.github.io/

- Screenshots are real captures: panel a from a local checkout of each `submitted-original` tag, panel b from the live site, at 1440×900 and 390×844.
- On desktop (`min-width: 900px`, fine pointer, no reduced-motion preference), the procedural 3D `Fracture to Framework` specimen fractures along outward face normals and snaps into four portal columns, and each figure pins while scroll wipes from a to b (GSAP ScrollTrigger, Three.js, and Lenis smooth scroll, self-hosted). On wide screens a range slider compares a and b for every visitor. Phones, reduced-motion visitors, and no-JS visitors get a zero-Three.js SVG specimen and complete side-by-side panels.
- No build step. Vendor files: GSAP 3.13.0 (GreenSock standard no-charge license), Lenis 1.3.4 (MIT), Three.js r170 (MIT). Font: Schibsted Grotesk (SIL OFL).
- The original submissions are preserved. These later revisions document the design decisions, sources, corrections and remaining limitations. Sahar Rao is responsible for what ships.
